What qualifies

The Breakthroughs category is deliberately sparse. Not every positive development belongs here.

Items are designated Breakthroughs by our Claude editorial filter only when they represent a genuine first — a record broken, a grand challenge solved, a clinical milestone achieved, or a capability unlocked that was previously considered years away.

A new model version is not a breakthrough. A new model that scores above human expert level on a major benchmark for the first time — that is.
